TMSpline

Draw the track.
Drive it in Trackmania.

TMSpline builds Trackmania roads from splines: drag points, bank the road, bend it into half-pipes and tubes, set width and borders per point, and send it straight into the game's editor.

The app

The editor for Windows. It exports maps on your own PC with Nadeo's NadeoImporter, which it downloads from Nadeo the first time you export.

The Openplanet plugin

Optional, inside Trackmania: reloads your track in the game's editor after every export (live update), and builds the block library so the app shows the game's real blocks and textures.

Put TMSpline.op into %USERPROFILE%\OpenplanetNext\Plugins. It runs in Openplanet's developer mode (Openplanet menu → Developer → Signature mode), as it is not published on openplanet.dev.

Getting started

  1. Install the app and sign in with this account: your tracks are saved here and follow you between PCs.
  2. Draw a track. Ctrl+click the ground to add points, drag them and their handles, Q/E banks.
  3. Export. Export map downloads a .Map.Gbx; Export to game puts it into your Maps folder.
  4. With the plugin: open the map in the game's editor once. After that every export reloads it there, and what you placed in the game's editor stays.
  5. Share a track from the app: everyone with an account can open the link and take a copy.
